5The minimum-payment trap

A credit-card balance is a loan you pay back as an annuity. Pay only the minimum and the payment shrinks as the balance shrinks, so the loan drags on for years. Pay a fixed amount and it is an ordinary annuity you can solve for N.

6The true cost of “easy” money

Short-term credit is usually advertised as a small fee: $15 per $100, a $7 late fee, “just $20 a week.” Turn each fee into a rate per period, then an annual rate. The APR simply multiplies the periodic rate by the number of periods a year, m. The effective annual rate (EAR) also counts interest on interest: EAR = (1 + APR/m)m − 1.
