Bond Price and Yield

A bond's price is the present value of its coupons and its par value, discounted at the required return rd. Raise rd and the same promised cash flows are worth less today. Price and yield sit on opposite ends of a seesaw.

Price by year

Prices are the present value of the coupons plus the present value of par, valued just after a coupon is paid. For semiannual bonds: N = years × 2, I/YR = rd ÷ 2, PMT = annual coupon ÷ 2; PV and FV are unchanged.
