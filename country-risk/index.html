Country Risk Premiums

Investors want extra return for putting money into a riskier country. Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern) turns each government's sovereign bond rating into a country risk premium (CRP) and adds it to the premium for a mature, low-risk market. Explore the map, then see how the premium raises the cost of equity for a project abroad.
