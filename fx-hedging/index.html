Hedge or Gamble?

A US firm will receive or pay foreign currency in 90 days, so it has transaction exposure. It can remain unhedged or use a forward hedge, a money market hedge or a currency option hedge. Compare what each one pays for every possible future spot rate, then replay real 90-day windows since 1999.

      4What to notice

      Simplifications: no bid–ask spreads; one borrowing rate equals one deposit rate in each currency; the option premium is paid today and, as in the Ch. 11 tables, its interest cost is ignored; options are European (exercised only at day 90). Forwards and option prices in section 3 are estimates or assumptions, not market quotes. Historical windows overlap (a new one starts every trading day), so their counts are not independent observations.
