3Ratios from the picture

Each ratio compares two pieces of the same income statement. Same firm as above.

Operating margin is measured before interest, so borrowing does not change it. Net profit margin and times interest earned both fall.

How it's calculated
Operating margin = EBIT ÷ Sales
Net profit margin = Net income ÷ Sales
Times interest earned = EBIT ÷ Interest
Payout ratio = Dividends ÷ Net income · Retention ratio = 1 − payout
Sources and notes

An example firm with round numbers, not a real company. The default 21% tax rate is the U.S. federal corporate rate since 2018.

Simplified: one tax rate, no tax refund in a loss year, no preferred stock, no other income. Order of payment follows the income statement: operating costs, interest, taxes, then the owners (dividends and retained earnings).
