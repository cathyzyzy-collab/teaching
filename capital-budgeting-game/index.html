NPV = PV of future CFs − cost. IRR is the rate where NPV = 0. Payback counts years until the undiscounted CFs repay the cost; discounted payback uses PVs at the WACC. PI = PV of future CFs ÷ initial cost, so PI > 1 exactly when NPV > 0.

    Reveal the numbers in step 1 first, so these points don't spoil the game.

      Classroom simulation: every startup, pitch and cash flow is invented. Cash flows arrive at the end of each year and are in $ millions. Projects are independent except the two that share a site, which are mutually exclusive. Decision rules follow the Module 8 slides: accept independent projects with NPV > 0 (equivalently IRR > WACC); for mutually exclusive projects, take the one with the higher positive NPV. With a capital budget, the best portfolio is the affordable combination with the largest total NPV, found here by checking every combination. Round codes rebuild the same random menu on any device.
